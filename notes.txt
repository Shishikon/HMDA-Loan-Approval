1. Logistic Regression

- Preprocessing: scaled.
- Purpose: establish your first actual predictive model.
- Helps understand how features influence classification probabilities.


2. Decision Tree Classifier
- Preprocessing: unscaled.
- Purpose: understand rule-based classification.
- Can capture nonlinear relationships.
- Easy to visualize and interpret.


3. Random Forest
- Preprocessing: unscaled.
- Purpose: combine multiple decision trees.
- Usually a strong starting point for tabular datasets


4. HistGradientBoostingClassifier
- Preprocessing: numerical imputation and categorical encoding.
- Purpose: learn complex patterns through sequential boosting.
- Useful for comparing different ensemble approaches.



5. Support Vector Machine (SVM)
- Preprocessing: scaled.
- Purpose: investigate classification boundaries.
- Can be computationally expensive on large datasets.



6. XGBoost or CatBoost
- Purpose: compare a specialized boosting algorithm.
- Often competitive on structured tabular data.
- CatBoost is particularly interesting when dealing with categorical features.
